Historical SAR–AIS comparison

What this click shows: delayed Sentinel-1 SAR detection cells together with the nearest GFW hourly AIS-presence cells for the same vessel identity, within the configured time tolerance. Current AIS, VOI and Danish vessel layers are automatically hidden. Historical AIS comparison is bounded, always prioritises Voodoo watchlist matches, and then uses the newest additional matched identities. Dashed lines connect the same GFW vessel identity; they are not tracks.

Sources and limits

Displayed vessel scope: harmonized priority VOIs plus neutral tanker context. The full harmonized traffic product remains available for download but is not drawn by default. Historical supplement: Danish land-based AIS, delayed and limited to two observations per vessel in a known Voodoo category. Satellite supplement: delayed Global Fishing Watch Sentinel-1 SAR vessel-detection report cells plus bounded GFW AIS Vessel Presence cells from the corresponding historical hours. Infrastructure: EMODnet Human Activities and original providers.

Coverage is mixed and not continuous. Older, repaired or delayed timestamps are visibly marked. SAR and historical GFW AIS coordinates are report-cell centres. The historical SAR–AIS mode automatically hides current vessel layers to prevent false time overlays.
