Sources and limits

Displayed vessel scope: harmonized priority VOIs plus neutral tanker context. Optional small background points show a capacity-limited selection of received traffic, not complete coverage. Historical supplement: Danish land-based AIS, delayed and limited to two observations per vessel in a known Voodoo category. Satellite supplement: delayed Global Fishing Watch Sentinel-1 SAR vessel-detection report cells plus bounded GFW AIS Vessel Presence cells from the corresponding historical hours. Infrastructure: EMODnet Human Activities and original providers.

Coverage is mixed and not continuous. Older, repaired or delayed timestamps are visibly marked. SAR and historical GFW AIS coordinates are report-cell centres. The historical SAR–AIS mode automatically hides current vessel layers to prevent false time overlays.
